Spood Beanie Ideas

Four embroidered cuff designs made to fit embroidery rules: bold letters, thick lines, and only 2–3 thread colors. Tap the dots to try each one on a different beanie color.

Designed to Fourthwall’s embroidery guidelines

  • At most 6 thread colors (these use 2–3)
  • Capital letters at least 0.3 in tall; lines and gaps at least 0.05 in
  • Solid shapes only: no gradients, shadows or tiny details
  • Sized for a front cuff area of about 5 × 2 in. Check the exact area in Fourthwall’s designer before ordering.

Tell Claude which one you like (A, B, C or D) and the beanie color. Claude will then make the finished embroidery file.
